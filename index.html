Our Services

Expertise at Competitive Rates

We provide a full range of demolition and hauling services. Whether your project is small or large scale, we guide you through every step so you always know what to expect.

From concrete removal to highly detailed selective demolition and hand excavation, DEMOMEX is equipped to handle challenging jobs safely and efficiently while keeping your project on schedule and within budget.

Service

Concrete Demolition & Hauling

We break up and haul away driveways, slabs, walkways, and other concrete structures, from small residential areas to larger commercial pours. Our crew brings the equipment and experience needed to keep the work area safe and clear.

We walk you through each step of the process so you understand how the concrete will be removed, recycled, or disposed of responsibly.

Service

Selective Demolition

From interior walls and finishes to portions of existing structures, we perform selective demolition with care so surrounding areas stay protected. Each project starts with a detailed walk through and clear scope.

We review access, utilities, and protection plans in advance so there are no surprises and the work stays on budget.

Service

Pier and Trench Excavation by Hand

When equipment access is limited or precision matters, we specialize in hand dug piers and trenches. This method protects existing utilities and structures while still keeping your project moving forward.

Our experience with tight sites allows us to offer this service at competitive rates without sacrificing a clean, professional finish.
